---
title: About the Council
description: What the Science and Technology Council is, and how to join it.
---

The Science and Technology Council is the technical society of IIT Mandi: eight
clubs and four cells covering programming, bioengineering, automotive
engineering, robotics, astronomy, mechanics, civil engineering, and
entrepreneurship.

It is a body of students, advised by faculty. Each club has a faculty advisor
and a co-advisor, and is run day to day by its coordinators and a core team.

Every club supports open participation. You can join at any point in the year,
you do not need prior experience, and dedicated members are given real
responsibility rather than a title. Members of the council also represent the
institute at national events — the medal record is on the
[achievements page](/achievements/).
