Raptor Racing designs, fabricates, and competes with a Formula E race car at
Formula Bharat 2026. The team is 27 students working under Dr. Sarthak Nag and
a group of mentors, split across mechanical, electrical, and documentation
work.

The car carries battery management, regenerative braking, and thermal
management systems. Building it means applying CAD, CAM, FEA, and CFD to a
problem where the answer either drives or does not.
