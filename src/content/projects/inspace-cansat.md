The team competes in the IN-SPACe CANSAT India Student Competition, organised by
the Astronautical Society of India and ISRO, designing and launching a
can-sized satellite weighing 1 kg to an altitude of 1000 metres.

The work runs in four stages: design and component selection, with preliminary
and final designs and budget planning; subsystem development and integration;
testing through to flight readiness review; and the launch itself, followed by
post-flight review.
