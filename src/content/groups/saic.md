S.A.I.C is the security and systems cell at IIT Mandi. The team competes in
Capture The Flag competitions and hosts and maintains campus websites.

If penetration testing interests you, the cell will teach you how to find
vulnerabilities and how the classic injection classes actually work — and put
you to work on a campus where security breaches become history.
