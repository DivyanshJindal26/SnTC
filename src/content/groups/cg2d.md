CG2D is a community for digital creativity and interactive entertainment. It
runs workshops, projects, and seminars on 3D modelling, animation, game design,
and real-time rendering.

Whether you are an experienced developer or starting from nothing, CG2D has the
resources and the people to get you further into computer graphics and game
development.
