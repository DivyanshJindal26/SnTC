E-Cell is the entrepreneurship club of IIT Mandi. An alumni initiative, it is
run by an interdisciplinary team under the mentorship of Dr. Satvasheel Powar,
working ideas outward until they reach the people they are meant to serve.

The club runs a wide spread of events built around business and its adjacent
disciplines.
