Robotics + Electronics = Robotronics. In the electronics domain the club works
across analog and digital, building practical skill in designing and debugging
circuits.

On the robotics side it supplies what you actually need to build a robot:
workspace, tools, components, funding, and people who have done it before and
will answer your questions.
