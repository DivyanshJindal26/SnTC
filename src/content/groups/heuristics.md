Heuristics works to build the culture of data science and machine learning at
IIT Mandi, extending into complex optimization problems.

Heuristics underlie the whole field of artificial intelligence and the
simulation of thinking — they are what you reach for when no known algorithm
applies. The cell runs regular events, challenges, and workshops.
