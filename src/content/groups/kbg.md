At the juncture of biology and engineering, KBG offers exposure to a wide
variety of topics: computational biology, genetics, neuroscience, biomechanics,
biotechnology, nanotechnology, biomimetics, and the branches that keep
diverging from the interplay between nature's craft and the desire to
understand it.
