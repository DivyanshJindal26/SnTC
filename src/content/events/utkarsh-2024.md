Utkarsh is the Science and Technology Council's own fest: three days in which
every club puts up something you can walk in and take part in, whether you have
built a robot before or have never opened a CAD file.

The 2024 edition ran eleven events across the North Campus — chassis built from
wooden sticks, bridges from ice cream sticks, rockets from plastic bottles,
robots fighting in the Village Square, and a scavenger hunt for a rogue
organism's genome.
